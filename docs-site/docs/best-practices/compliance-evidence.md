# Compliance Evidence

Good evidence is specific, dated, attributable, and tied to a control.

## Evidence checklist

::: steps
1. Record the tenant, subject, actor, and timestamp.
2. Store the AI use case or workflow name.
3. Link to the relevant AI Act or GDPR article.
4. Preserve the decision and state transition.
5. Keep mitigation notes close to the incident or review record.
:::

## Evidence quality

| Weak | Strong |
| --- | --- |
| "AI issue fixed" | "High refusal drift for `tenant_id=acme` mitigated by prompt policy v3" |
| "User deleted" | "DSAR erasure completed for verified subject with retained billing exception" |
| "Human approved" | "Reviewer approved generated decision after checking source documents" |

::: callout tip "Audit posture"
Write notes as if the reader is a future auditor who does not know your product.
:::
